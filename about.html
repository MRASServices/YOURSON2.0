Your Son was born in 2024 and created with the express purpose of making life easier for the individuals, families, and professionals that we work with.

We can be described as full-time fixers; a jack of every trade; or an oddly pleasant mix of handyman and personal assistant, with a lot of other capabilities in between.

However you describe us, what matters most is that we get things done:

Properly. Earnestly. Transparently.

We act as an extension of you, and afford our clients the ability to be in more than one place at a time.

Whether it's tasks in your home, work in your yard, or errands around town, Your Son makes outsourcing those outstanding to-do's easy.

In an effort to encompass all that we do, and the way that we do it, we simply say that we are:

Like having a son, except we actually listen

So why not give us a call? Your Son would love to hear from you!

Let’s figure it out together

Your Son's Approach

Proper

We were raised to believe that the only proper way to go about doing something is by doing it the right way.

Otherwise, why bother?

We take pride in our work and derive our satisfaction from the happiness and peace of mind we afford our clients.

Earnest

When we make a promise, we intend to keep it.

For Your Son, each client and every request is considered of equal importance.

Simply put, this means that we won't disappear on you in lieu of other opportunities.

For us, that shows nothing more than poor planning and insincerity.

Transparent

We've worked in corporate Canada before.

Notice the past tense?

We understand the value and know the importance of being forthright to our clients.

From our performance to our price, we prefer to keep it provably honest.
