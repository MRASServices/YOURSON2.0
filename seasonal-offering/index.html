OUR PRICING

Whether it's a light dusting or a proper Canadian snowfall, we offer straightforward pricing based on the amount of snow and the areas requiring clearing.

Prices per clearing
ServiceUnder 5 cm5–15 cmOver 15–25 cm
Walkway$20$35$45
Standard 2-car driveway$30$50$70
Driveway & walkway$50$65$90

A few things worth knowing:

  • Under 5 cm: Available by request only. Reduced rates apply.
  • 5–15 cm: Our regular automatic clearing service.
  • Over 15–25 cm: Heavy snowfall pricing applies, subject to prior authorization.
  • Over 25 cm: Individually quoted and approved before service.

All prices are per clearing and apply to standard-sized areas. Your individual quote will confirm the areas included.

Snowfall amounts refer to approximate new accumulation since the last clearing. Isolated drifts and municipal windrows are assessed separately. Heavy snowfall prices include the surcharge, which requires advance approval.

Additional Services

Municipal Snowplow Windrow Removal — $20

If the municipal plow leaves a windrow after we've already cleared your driveway, a separate return visit can be arranged for $20, subject to availability.

Normal windrows already present when we arrive are included in standard driveway clearing. Exceptionally large, compacted, or icy windrows may require a separate quote.

Salt & Ice Treatment — Available Upon Request

Additional salting and ice treatment services are available, with pricing determined by the area and conditions.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Get on the Route

    Get in touch and tell us a little about your property. We'll review your requirements, confirm your pricing, and discuss how the service operates.

    Once you're enrolled, you're part of our regular neighbourhood route.

  2. Let It Snow

    When approximately 5 cm (2 inches) of snow has accumulated, our automatic service is activated.

    You don't need to call, book a visit, or remind us. We'll take care of scheduling your clearing.

    For snowfalls under 5 cm, simply contact us if you'd like a clearing.

  3. We'll Take It From There

    We'll make our way through the neighbourhood, clearing the areas included in your service arrangement.

    A courtesy notification will let you know when our route is activated, and we'll confirm when your clearing has been completed.

  4. Pay Per Clearing

    No upfront seasonal payment. No paying for snow that never arrives.

    You pay only for the services performed, at the prices agreed upon in advance.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much snow must fall before you come?

Our automatic service begins when approximately 5 cm (2 inches) of snow has accumulated. Once you're enrolled, there's no need to request a visit each time.

What if we get less than 5 cm of snow?

We also offer light snow clearing at reduced rates. These visits are available by request only and are subject to scheduling availability.

Depending on conditions, we may use a shovel or leaf blower rather than a snowblower.

What time will my snow be cleared?

We operate on a neighbourhood-based route, clearing properties as efficiently as weather conditions allow.

Arrival times depend on the amount of snowfall, road conditions, and the work required at each property. Although specific completion times cannot be guaranteed, we'll do our best to keep you informed.

What happens if it snows all day?

During prolonged snowfall, we'll assess conditions and determine when clearing will be most effective.

Our regular service generally includes one clearing per snowfall event. If additional visits are required, they'll be arranged and authorized separately.

What neighbourhoods do you service?

Our snow clearing route is focused on the Stonehaven neighbourhood in Newmarket, particularly the Lockwood Circle and Secretariate Road area.

If you're nearby, feel free to get in touch. We'll be happy to let you know whether your property can be accommodated.

What happens during particularly heavy snowfall?

A little snow is one thing. A whole lot of snow is another!

Our regular prices cover accumulations from 5 cm through 15 cm.

For accumulations over 15 cm and up to 25 cm, an additional charge applies:

  • Walkway: $10
  • Driveway: $20
  • Driveway & walkway: $25

These amounts can be pre-authorized when you enrol, so your automatic service can continue without interruption.

For accumulations exceeding 25 cm, we'll provide an individual quote and obtain approval before proceeding.

Do you offer salting and ice treatment?

Yes! Salt and ice treatment are available as additional services.

Because weather conditions can change quickly, cleared or treated surfaces may still become slippery. We'll discuss suitable options for your property.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all! As long as we have safe access to the areas requiring clearing, we'll take care of the work while you carry on with your day.

What if the municipal snowplow comes through after you've finished?

Ah, the dreaded snowplow!

If the plow has already passed when we arrive, removal of a normal windrow is included in your driveway clearing.

If it comes through after we've completed your property, a separate return clearing can be arranged for $20, subject to availability.

Exceptionally large or icy windrows may require a separate quote.

What if my vehicle is parked in the driveway?

No problem! We'll clear the reasonably accessible areas around your vehicle.

For the most thorough clearing, we recommend moving vehicles beforehand whenever possible. Areas blocked by vehicles or other obstacles may remain uncleared.

What areas of my property are included?

We offer snow clearing for driveways, walkways, and other agreed areas of your property.

Your individual quote will identify exactly what's included, so there are no misunderstandings about the work being performed.

Can I skip a clearing if I don't need it?

Of course! Simply let us know before our route has been activated for that snowfall.

We'll do our best to accommodate later requests, although changes may not always be possible once the route is underway.

Can I pause or discontinue the service?

Absolutely. We don't believe in keeping customers tied to a service they no longer need.

We appreciate 24 hours' notice whenever possible so we can adjust our route. There are no seasonal cancellation penalties, and you'll only be responsible for authorized services already completed.

Do I have to pay for the entire winter?

Nope! Our service operates on a pay-per-clearing basis.

No upfront seasonal payment, and no charges when no service is performed.

Why do you only accept a limited number of customers?

Because we'd rather provide dependable service to a small number of neighbours than spread ourselves too thin.

We're limiting our route to approximately 20 properties so we can keep our service local, manageable, and personal.
