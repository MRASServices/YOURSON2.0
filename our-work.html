While we do a litany of other things that keep us well clear of dirt, sawdust, and general grime - it tends to be the things that make us dirty and uncomfortable that produce results worth photographing.

So while we may do more than the select view memories below, we believe these to be worth cataloging - and most importantly, sharing with you.

After all, seeing is believing - and also, who even wants to see photos of an organized CRM system, a new website build, or a time-sensative delivery - how do you even photograph that? Ah, who cares, just look at the pictures:
